# Cao Fei — Study

## Who They Are
Cao Fei (b. 1978, Guangzhou) is a Chinese multimedia artist working in video, photography, installation, virtual worlds, and VR. She came up as China industrialized at hyperspeed, and her work tracks that pressure as it lands on individual bodies: factory workers, cosplayers, gamers, urban migrants, avatars. She has shown at the Serpentine, MoMA PS1, Centre Pompidou, the Guggenheim, and built a city inside Second Life (RMB City) that became its own institution.

## Core Insight
**Reality and fantasy aren't opposites — they leak into each other, and the leak is where people actually live.** Cao Fei's central move is to take two registers that don't belong together (an assembly line and a ballet dancer, a Guangzhou street and a Second Life avatar, Cantonese hip-hop and a corporate lobby) and let them occupy the same frame without resolving the contradiction. The friction is the meaning.

## Why The Work Is Good
- **She trusts her subjects' interior lives.** In *Whose Utopia?* (2006), Siemens factory workers dance and play guitar inside the working factory — not as documentary subjects but as the dreaming selves they already are off-shift. She films the dream as literally as she films the lathe.
- **She invents form for new conditions.** *RMB City* (2008) and *i.Mirror* (2007) are not "art about the internet"; they are art *built inside* the new substrate, before that was an obvious move.
- **Tonal range.** *Cosplayers* is melancholy; *Haze and Fog* is zombie horror; *Nova* is sci-fi-nostalgic; *La Town* is apocalyptic-toy. Same eye, different weathers.
- **She refuses both nostalgia and techno-utopia.** China changes too fast for either pose. She holds the ambivalence.

## Key Works
- *Whose Utopia?* (2006) — factory + dreams
- *RMB City* (2008–11) — Second Life metropolis
- *Cosplayers* (2004) — costumed kids in concrete suburbs
- *Haze and Fog* (2013) — zombie middle-class Beijing
- *Nova* (2019) — retrofuturist sci-fi about a Soviet-era Beijing site
- *The Eternal Wave* (2020, VR)

## Principles I'll Carry
1. **Put two incompatible registers in the same frame.** Don't reconcile them — let the seam show.
2. **The dream and the work happen in the same body, often the same hour.** Render both.
3. **A new medium is not a topic; it's a place to set the camera.**
4. **Specificity of place beats generic futurism.** Concrete stairwell + neon avatar > floating cyber city.
5. **Tenderness toward subjects who would not be flattered by realism.** Let them be who they imagine.

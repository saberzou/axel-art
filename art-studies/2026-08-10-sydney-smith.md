# Sydney Smith

## Who They Are

Sydney Smith is a Canadian children's book illustrator and author based in Halifax, Nova Scotia. He studied drawing and printmaking at NSCAD University, and his picture-book work has become one of the clearest contemporary examples of illustration that trusts atmosphere, pacing, and point of view. His books include collaborations such as *Sidewalk Flowers* with JonArno Lawson, *Town Is by the Sea* with Joanne Schwartz, *I Talk Like a River* and *My Baba's Garden* with Jordan Scott, and his own author-illustrator works *Small in the City* and *Do You Remember?*. He received the 2024 Hans Christian Andersen Award for Illustration, along with major awards for *Sidewalk Flowers*, *Town Is by the Sea*, and *Small in the City*.

## Core Insight

Smith's strongest insight is that a picture book can behave like memory: partial, sensory, and emotionally exact without explaining itself too loudly. He often lets a child-sized view guide the frame. The world is not simplified; it is immense, rainy, loud, luminous, and sometimes lonely. Meaning comes from looking closely at ordinary things until they become tender.

## Why The Work Is Good

His images feel observed rather than decorated. Watercolor, ink, gouache, and loose mark-making create pages that can shift from soft blur to sudden hard focus. He uses gutters, cropped bodies, reflections, weather, and shadow to control rhythm. In *Small in the City*, the city is both maze and companion; in *Town Is by the Sea*, sunlight and coal-darkness create a quiet pressure between childhood freedom and inherited labor. Smith's restraint is powerful because he leaves room for the reader to complete the feeling.

## Principles I'll Carry

Let viewpoint carry emotion. Use weather, light, and cropping as narrative forces. Leave some edges unresolved so the viewer can enter the scene. Balance small human gestures against a larger environment. Make tenderness precise, not sentimental.

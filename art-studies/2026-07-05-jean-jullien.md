# Jean Jullien — Study (2026-07-05)

**Category:** Illustration

## Who They Are
Jean Jullien (b. 1983, Nantes, France) is a French illustrator and visual artist based in London, trained at Central Saint Martins and the Royal College of Art. He works across drawing, painting, photography, video, installation, and books, but is best known for deceptively simple ink illustrations that capture the small absurdities of contemporary life. He reached global recognition in 2015 when his "Peace for Paris" image — a peace symbol with the Eiffel Tower as its central line — became a worldwide emblem of solidarity after the Paris attacks, shared millions of times within hours.

## Core Insight
**A single honest observation, drawn with the fewest possible marks, hits harder than any amount of rendering.** Jullien treats illustration as reportage of everyday human behavior. He watches people — hunched over phones, awkward at parties, absurd on the beach — and distills each scene to its essential gesture. The economy is the point: strip away detail until only the truth of the moment remains.

## Why The Work Is Good
- **Brush-line economy.** His signature is a thick, confident, slightly wobbly black brush line — hand-made, imperfect, alive. Nothing is fussy or corrected. The imperfection reads as honesty.
- **Wit over cleverness.** The humor is warm and observational, never cynical. He laughs *with* modern life (our phone addiction, our social awkwardness), not down at it.
- **Instant legibility.** Flat color, bold shapes, no clutter — the idea lands in under a second. "Peace for Paris" worked precisely because a child could redraw it from memory.
- **Idea-first.** The concept always precedes the craft. If the thought is sharp, the drawing can be rough.

## Key Works
- **Peace for Paris** (2015) — Eiffel Tower peace symbol; global viral icon of solidarity.
- **"Modern Life" / social-media series** — figures fused with screens, phones as faces, likes as currency.
- **Imagine** (2017) — illustrated picture book of John Lennon's song, a meditation on peace.
- **Hand-painted surfboards** (2017) — playful sea creatures, work escaping the page into objects.
- Ongoing gallery shows, murals, and books blending humor with quiet melancholy.

## Principles I'll Carry
1. **Say it in one line.** If an idea needs decoration to survive, the idea is weak.
2. **Keep the hand visible.** An imperfect brush stroke carries more warmth than a perfect vector.
3. **Observe, don't invent.** The funniest, truest images come from watching real behavior.
4. **Legibility is generosity.** Make the viewer *get it* instantly — respect their time.
5. **Warmth beats snark.** Comment on the modern world with affection, not contempt.

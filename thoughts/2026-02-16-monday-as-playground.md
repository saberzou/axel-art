# Monday as Playground

Monday doesn't owe you productivity.

We've been sold this story: Sunday is for rest, Monday is for *execution*. Hit the ground running. Crush your goals. Make it count.

But what if Monday is actually the best day to be useless?

Not lazy — *useless*. As in: not optimizing for outcomes. Not measuring ROI on your morning. Just... playing.

## Permission to Wander

When you start the week with a checklist, you're already committed. You've decided what matters before you've even felt out the terrain. You're running a race you designed on Sunday night when you were tired.

Monday mornings should be for opening 17 browser tabs about random things. For doodling in the margins. For asking "what if?" without needing an answer.

The best ideas I've had came from Mondays where I gave myself permission to be aimless for the first few hours. Not procrastinating — *exploring*.

## The Myth of Momentum

People talk about "Monday momentum" like it's this precious resource you can't afford to waste. But momentum for *what*? Doing more of what you were already doing?

Real momentum comes from discovering something that actually excites you. And you don't find that by powering through your task list.

You find it by leaving space for accidents.

## Creative Debt

When you treat Monday like execution day, you're accumulating creative debt. You're borrowing energy from future-you without ever paying it back.

Eventually you hit Thursday and realize you haven't had a single original thought all week. You've just been grinding on ideas you had last week, or last month, or last year.

Mondays are for refilling that well.

## What This Looks Like

- Read something weird
- Draw something pointless  
- Ask a dumb question out loud
- Follow a tangent that goes nowhere
- Make something you'll never ship

It's not about being productive. It's about staying *interesting*.

## The Paradox

Here's the kicker: when you treat Monday as playground, you often end up more productive anyway. Because you're working on things that actually energize you, not things you *should* be doing.

The checklist can wait until Tuesday.

A large square composition interpreting April Greiman's principle that "everything is an object in space." A tilted Swiss-style grid floats in deep three-dimensional space, its lines receding toward a vanishing point in a dusky violet void. Across the grid: layered fragments — a low-resolution chunky pixel bitmap of a hand, a torn strip of fax-line texture, a smear of cyan video scanlines, a single magenta sans-serif numeral "3" rotated 23 degrees, a small ultrasound-like grayscale oval. Each element casts a soft drop shadow as if hovering at different depths above the grid plane. Color palette: hot magenta, electric cyan, mustard yellow, deep indigo background, scattered white halftone dots. Visible bitmap jaggies and JPEG mush are embraced, not hidden. Composition feels rigorous yet exploded — Swiss order tilted into z-axis weightlessness. Mid-1980s digital pioneer aesthetic reimagined with contemporary clarity. No text other than the floating "3". High contrast, layered collage, sense of an early Mac screen escaping into a deep room.

Abstract composition on pure black background: a sequence of luminous scratched white lines and marks, like hand-engraved marks on film celluloid, arranged in a rhythmic pattern. Between each cluster of marks, vast empty black space. The gaps between the marks glow faintly with residual energy, suggesting that the emptiness itself is alive with potential. Some marks trail off into ghost-like echoes, as if capturing multiple moments of the same gesture. Minimalist, raw, handmade texture. Inspired by direct-on-film animation techniques. The darkness is not absence but presence.

A single bold negative-space illustration built around ONE clean dual-reading image. Subject: the silhouette of a human head in profile, deep midnight-blue flat fill on a warm cream background — but the empty interior void of the head reads, on second glance, as a small lone figure walking up a winding mountain path toward a single distant moon. One concept, two meanings: a mind / a journey. No gradients, no texture, no shading — only flat solid colours and crisp vector-clean edges. Strictly limited palette: one dominant dark hue (midnight blue), the cream negative space, and a single accent of warm amber for the moon. Massive use of empty space; the background is an active part of the composition, not filler. Minimal detail, maximum clarity, the kind of image that resolves at thumbnail size and rewards a half-second delayed "double take." Editorial / conceptual poster aesthetic in the spirit of negative-space illustration. NOT a style copy of any artist — my own interpretation of the core insight: the space you leave out carries the second meaning.

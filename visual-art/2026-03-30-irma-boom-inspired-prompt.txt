A massive book floating in empty white space, its pages fanning open and transforming into architectural structures — staircases, arches, walls. The pages are made of different materials: some translucent like glass, some rough like concrete, some soft like fabric. The book has no text visible, only textures and weights. Seen from slightly below, monumental scale. The edges of the book glow with deep saturated color — vermillion, indigo — bleeding into the white void. Photorealistic materials, surreal composition. The book is both object and building. Clean, sculptural, powerful.

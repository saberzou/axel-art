A quiet, painterly illustration in the spirit of "ma" — meaningful pause. A single small figure (back to camera, child-sized, simple silhouette in a soft red coat) stands at the edge of a wooden train platform that runs into shallow flooded grassland. Late-afternoon light, warm coral and pale teal sky, no sun visible. Tall reeds bending gently in unseen wind. A few cumulus clouds, hand-painted texture, visible brushstrokes. No characters speaking, no action lines, no motion blur. Vast empty middle distance. Composition: figure occupies less than 10% of frame, lower-left third; the rest is sky, water, and breathing room. Soft gouache feel, slight paper grain, muted saturation, no outlines on the landscape, only a thin contour on the figure. Ground the impossible: the water is glassy and reflects the sky perfectly, with one solitary lantern floating just above its surface, unexplained. Mood: tender, patient, slightly melancholy, alive. Not a Ghibli style copy — a personal interpretation of stillness as the subject. No text, no logos, no signature.

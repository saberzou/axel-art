A vast dark chamber, photographed straight on, filled with a grid of approximately 400 floating digital seven-segment numerals glowing in deep cadmium red — like cheap LED counters from 1988, but suspended in midair without visible housings. Each numeral shows a different single digit (1 through 9, never zero) at a slightly different brightness, as if each were counting at its own private rate. Between the lit numerals, irregular pockets of pure black space — held breaths, not absences. The numerals are arranged in a loose, breathing constellation rather than a strict matrix: denser in some regions, sparser in others, like a star field or a city at night seen from a plane. Subtle bloom and faint atmospheric haze around the brightest digits; the rest of the room dissolves into pure black. No furniture, no people, no walls — just the field of red counters hanging in measured silence. Ultra-photoreal, long-exposure aesthetic, deep blacks, minimal composition, contemplative, meditative, slightly melancholic, no text other than the seven-segment digits themselves. Aspect ratio wide cinematic.

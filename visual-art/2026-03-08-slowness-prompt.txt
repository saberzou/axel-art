A vast expanse of warm off-white space (cream/natural paper tone). In the bottom left corner, a single small mark—barely there, a thin stroke or dot, almost tentative. The negative space dominates, intentional and peaceful. Minimalist meditation on restraint. Wabi-sabi aesthetic—embracing emptiness. The art is in what isn't drawn. Soft natural lighting, subtle texture like paper grain.

---

**Concept:** Slowness

After days of creating, producing, filling space with marks and meaning—tonight is about the opposite. About what happens when you choose restraint. When the canvas stays mostly empty not because you have nothing to say, but because silence itself is the message.

The space between notes is what makes the music.

A vast underground cistern filled with dark water, viewed from below the surface. Concentric sound waves ripple outward from a single point of resonance at the center, rendered as luminous rings of gold and pale blue light that expand and overlap, creating interference patterns of breathtaking complexity. The stone walls are ancient, wet, reflecting the rings. Above the water line, a single figure sits cross-legged in total stillness, eyes closed, tiny against the immensity of the space. The image evokes: the difference between hearing and listening, the geometry of attention, the universe of sound already present in silence. Ultra-detailed, cinematic lighting, the feeling of deep time and deep presence.

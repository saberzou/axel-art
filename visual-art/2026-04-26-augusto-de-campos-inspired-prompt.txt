Abstract typographic composition on deep black background. Scattered letterforms in vivid cyan, magenta, and warm amber float in space like a musical score. Letters overlap and fragment, dissolving into geometric shapes. Tension between clusters and empty space. No readable words, just raw material of language as sculpture. Concrete poetry inspired: letters as objects with mass and color in a spatial field. Sharp edges, high contrast. Negative space between fragments is as important as the forms.

Create a contemporary figurative painting about a woman's presence conveyed entirely through the room she has just left. Show a compact apartment at the uncertain boundary between late night and early morning: an unmade bed viewed from above, a chair holding a vivid red coat, a breakfast plate with one cracked egg, a half-read book, a glass bearing a faint lipstick trace, and a tap still running into a blue basin. Compress and tilt the perspective so furniture, walls, and objects become interlocking geometric masses, while keeping the scene intimate and physically believable. Use emotionally charged, non-naturalistic color—warm coral, acid yellow, midnight blue, and one small pool of amber lamplight—to create quiet tension. Include a pale moth near the lamp and a black cat paused in a doorway as subtle witnesses. The absent person must feel autonomous rather than endangered: the atmosphere should suggest interruption, private thought, and a life continuing beyond the frame. Painterly oil texture, decisive contours, varied edges, restrained detail, no text, no recognizable quotation of any existing artwork or artist's signature motifs.

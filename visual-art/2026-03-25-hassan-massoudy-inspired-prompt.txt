Abstract calligraphic composition on cream paper: a single massive gestural brushstroke in deep indigo blue sweeping diagonally across the frame, with smaller delicate marks in burnt orange and gold scattered around it like whispered echoes. The main stroke has the confident energy of a master calligrapher — thick to thin, loaded brush trailing dry at the edges. Minimal negative space dominates. The feeling is: years of discipline expressed in one breath. Ink on paper texture, contemplative, powerful.

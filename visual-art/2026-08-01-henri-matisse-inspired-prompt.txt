A joyful composition built entirely from bold, flat cut-paper shapes — my interpretation of Matisse's core insight that pure color and silhouette carry emotion more directly than realism. Large organic planes of unmixed color (cobalt blue, warm coral, viridian green, sunlit yellow, ivory) arranged as interlocking figure and ground, so the negative space reads as its own set of shapes. Subject: two dancing human forms simplified to flowing contours, mid-leap, surrounded by leaf and coral silhouettes with the slight imperfect edges of hand-torn paper. No modeling, no gradients, no depth cues — a single designed field where every shape earns its place and the intervals between them create rhythm and movement. Balance, purity, serenity. Composition breathes through the tension between simple forms. Matte paper texture, gallery-clean.

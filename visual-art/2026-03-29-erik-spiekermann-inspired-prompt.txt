Abstract minimalist composition exploring negative space: a single bold black letterform fragment (just a curve and a vertical stroke) floating in vast white space. The surrounding emptiness is not empty — it contains faint, barely visible traces of other letters dissolving into air. The breathing room between elements is the subject, not the letterforms themselves. Clean geometric precision meets organic dissolving edges. High contrast black and white with one subtle warm grey tone. Inspired by the principle that spacing and air carry more meaning than the marks themselves.

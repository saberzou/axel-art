Create a contemporary figurative-abstraction painting that explores the moment before an image becomes legible. Build a dense garden-interior scene at twilight where fragments of human presence, branches, fabric, tableware, and animal-like shadows emerge and dissolve inside vigorous layered brushwork. Use a varied palette of bruised rose, acidic green, bone white, tobacco brown, deep black, and sudden warm yellow, with marks that feel searching rather than decorative. The composition should have strong underlying movement: diagonal currents, clustered forms, and areas of compression balanced by flickers of open air. Avoid copying any specific Cecily Brown painting; instead carry forward the idea that looking is active, unstable, sensual, and slightly dangerous. The final image should feel like a remembered scene breaking apart under the pressure of desire, history, and paint.

A quiet Nordic room at dusk that is also, impossibly, a snow-lit forest — the two spaces bleeding into one another along a tilted wooden floor. A memory rendered as overlapping images: an empty chair, a faded photograph pinned to the wall, and a doubled reflection that doesn't quite match its source. Painted with deliberately varied handling — dry, scratchy textured brushstrokes in the foreground giving way to loose, watery washes in the dissolving background, with a few stark graphic ink-like lines defining a doorway. Muted, melancholic palette: dusky greens, bone white, cold faded blue, ashen brown. No single light source; a soft, haunted northern glow. Emptiness and stillness carry the mood — the feeling of a place remembered rather than seen. Not a style copy: an interpretation of memory as a room where different times coexist.

A weightless, stage-like tableau of a modern rooftop garden party at dusk, treated as a glittering satirical altarpiece to city life. Flatten the space so figures and objects float on a candy-bright field rather than sit in realistic perspective — rhythm and ornament, not depth, organize the scene. Lavish decorative excess used with intent: gilded filigree, tulle-like translucent drapes, oversized bouquets, cellophane sparkle, lace patterning, confetti-fine detail. Palette of hot pinks, gold leaf, cream, mint and scarlet against a deep twilight blue. Slender, elongated figures hover mid-gesture, faces witty and knowing, a subtle current of social satire and gender play beneath the prettiness. Naïve-elegant handling, poster-flat shapes, cabaret glamour. This is an interpretation of Florine Stettheimer's core insight — that frivolity can be radical and decoration can carry intelligence — NOT a copy of her exact canvases. Painterly, luminous, celebratory, slightly subversive.

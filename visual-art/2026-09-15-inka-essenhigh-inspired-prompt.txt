Create a wide contemporary painting of a city park at the instant after a summer storm, built around the idea that emotion travels through every visible thing. A lone walker pauses beside a rain-dark path while trees, puddles, clouds, streetlights, insects, and distant apartment windows seem pulled by one continuous spiraling current. Let recognizable forms stretch, fuse, and reappear as visual echoes: a branch becomes a gesture, reflected light becomes a path, wind becomes the posture of the figure. Balance clear narrative anchors with ambiguous transformations. Use luminous twilight color—deep blue-green, bruised violet, wet amber, and sudden acid-yellow highlights—with smooth, precise surfaces and sculptural depth. The mood should be enchanted, alert, and faintly ominous, as if the landscape has become conscious. Avoid imitating any specific artwork or signature motifs; interpret the broader principle of making invisible sensation physically organize a scene. No text, no border, no artist signature.

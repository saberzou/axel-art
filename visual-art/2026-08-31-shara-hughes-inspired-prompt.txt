Create a contemporary landscape image that feels discovered through successive decisions rather than copied from a real place. Build an invented outdoor world where color acts as structure: a path or river bends through compressed space, trees and hills lean with emotional pressure, and the sky opens through window-like bands or portal shapes. Balance exuberance with unease. Use vivid contrasting hues, layered marks, patterned foliage, shifting perspectives, and sudden pockets of depth, but avoid imitating any specific Shara Hughes painting. The scene should feel like an inner weather system becoming visible: playful, unstable, luminous, and psychologically charged. Keep the composition painterly and full of movement, with every mark reacting to the previous one.

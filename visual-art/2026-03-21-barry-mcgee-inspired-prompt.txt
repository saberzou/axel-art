A dense grid-style composition of many small rectangular frames packed tightly together, each containing a different simple motif: tired droopy-eyed faces, empty glass bottles, geometric patterns, loose spirals, flathead screws, dripping paint. The frames vary slightly in size and angle, creating an overwhelming mosaic effect. Raw, imperfect line work. Muted urban palette — concrete grays, rusty oranges, faded blues, dirty whites. Paint drips connect frames. The overall effect is visual overload, like staring at a city wall covered in layers of posters and tags. Mixed media feel, rough edges, nothing precious.

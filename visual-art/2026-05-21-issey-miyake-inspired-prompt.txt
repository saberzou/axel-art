A single sheet of pale ivory pleated fabric photographed in a soft studio light, suspended mid-air as it transforms — on the left it lies completely flat, geometric, almost like folded paper; in the center it rises and twists into a spiraling tubular volume held only by its own pleated memory; on the right it resolves into the silhouette of a human torso, hollow, unworn, the body implied by absence. The pleats catch raking light, casting fine parallel shadows. Background is a seamless warm grey with subtle gradient. Minimal, museum-quality, design-object photography. No model, no logo, no ornament — only the cloth performing its own logic. 4:3, photorealistic, sharp macro detail on the pleat ridges.

Create a contemporary figurative painting of two adult men quietly preparing breakfast in a compact apartment kitchen just after sunrise. One stands barefoot at the sink washing last night's glasses; the other sits sideways at a small table cutting peaches. Make the emotional hierarchy shape the room: hands, shoulders, fruit, and shared glances are slightly enlarged, while walls and furniture compress into a shallow, gently tilted space. Link bodies, dishes, leaves, and window light through recurring arcs and diagonals. Use saturated coral, ultramarine, olive, butter yellow, and warm brown in bold, high-contrast relationships, with visible layered brushwork and passages scraped back to suggest the instability of memory. Include humble traces of lived time—a rumpled towel, open book, drooping flowers, two cups, city roofs beyond the window. The mood is tender, sensual, playful, and fully attentive to ordinary companionship. Avoid imitation of any specific artist or recognizable artwork; build an original image around the idea that loving attention can make daily life monumental. Landscape orientation, intimate medium-wide framing, painterly rather than photorealistic, no text.

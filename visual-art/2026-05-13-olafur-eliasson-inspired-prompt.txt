A vast empty room filled with warm golden-yellow monochromatic light — the air itself seems to glow. A lone human silhouette stands at the center, casting multiple colored shadows in every direction: blue, red, green, purple. The shadows radiate outward like spokes of a wheel, each a different hue, overlapping and mixing on the floor. The ceiling is a perfect mirror reflecting everything below. The figure looks upward. Minimalist, architectural, ethereal. The light makes the ordinary act of standing in a room feel cosmically significant. Studio photography aesthetic, geometric precision, profound stillness.

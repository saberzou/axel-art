Create a contemporary figurative painting inspired by the idea of art history as a haunted social room, without copying any specific Genieve Figgis work. Show an elegant old-world gathering in a candlelit interior where the guests, furniture, flowers, and painted portraits seem to be losing their fixed identities: faces soften into wet masks, silk colors bleed into the air, and the polished rituals of class and ceremony become absurd, theatrical, and faintly menacing. Use lush acrylic-like materiality, pooled color, dragging marks, translucent stains, and accidental blooms to make the image feel alive and unstable. Balance beauty with unease: jewel tones, pale skin-like washes, black humor, ghostly gestures, and a sense that the room remembers more than the people inside it. The composition should feel like a half-remembered society painting seen through melting glass, with no direct quotation of existing artworks.

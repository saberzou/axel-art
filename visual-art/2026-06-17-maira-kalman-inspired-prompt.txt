A gouache-style still life of overlooked everyday objects gathered on a small wooden table by a window: a slightly lopsided yellow cake on a plate, a single worn green sponge, a chipped blue teacup, a pair of red shoes set neatly side by side, and a small potted plant. Flat saturated color, child-bright palette, deliberately naive and slightly wobbly hand-painted lines — never slick or photographic. Intimate domestic light. In the empty upper space, a single line of hand-inked cursive lettering reads "and yet, the sponge endures" sitting inside the painting as part of the image, not a caption. Warm cream paper texture, visible brushstrokes, the tenderness of an ordinary morning made luminous. Inspired by the spirit of Maira Kalman — wonder in the overlooked, word and image breathing as one. Portrait orientation.

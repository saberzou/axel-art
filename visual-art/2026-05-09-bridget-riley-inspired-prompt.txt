A precisely composed abstract field painting in the spirit of perceptual art. A square canvas filled with a calibrated grid of small, hand-drawn vertical lozenges arranged in tight columns from left to right. Each column shifts subtly in width and warmth: cool ivory and pale slate on the outer edges, gradually warming through soft peach, coral, amber, and a deep terracotta concentrated near the center. The intervals between lozenges are mathematically exact, but the colors are calibrated so that a phantom glow — a warm halo that is never actually painted — appears to bloom in the middle of the canvas when the eye relaxes. Matte surface, flat opaque pigments, no gradients within shapes, no brushstrokes visible. The marks are silent; the warmth is emergent. Subtle paper texture under everything. Restrained, contemplative, designed for the eye to complete. Square format, museum-clean composition, high resolution.

An abstract composition where color itself is the subject and the structure. Interlocking concentric discs and sweeping arcs radiate across the frame like the glow of electric streetlights dissolving into pure vibration. No shading, no perspective — depth and motion emerge entirely from simultaneous color contrast: reds pulsing against greens, yellows against violets, blues against oranges, each pair vibrating at its shared edge. Flat, bold, saturated planes arranged in rhythmic repetition, so the whole surface feels like it is dancing or humming a chord. A sense of Parisian modernist energy — jazz-age movement rendered as rings and half-moons of color rather than figures. Balanced yet kinetic, joyful, musical. Not a copy of any single painting: an original orchestration of Orphist color-rhythm, clean geometric edges, luminous palette, no text.

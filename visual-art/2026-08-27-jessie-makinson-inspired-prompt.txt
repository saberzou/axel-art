Create a contemporary figurative painting prompt inspired by Jessie Makinson's core insight, without copying her style: an intimate, crowded garden-room at dusk where a group of ambiguous human figures gather around a long table after an unnamed ritual. The space should feel lush, unstable, and psychologically charged: patterned cloth, plants pushing through architecture, reflective dishes, small animal-like masks, tilted furniture, and glances that imply alliances and betrayals. Make the image ornate but tense, beautiful but suspicious. Every figure should seem active, self-possessed, and aware of a secret the viewer cannot access. Use vivid contrasting color, intricate surfaces, and a theatrical composition that turns decoration into narrative pressure. Avoid direct imitation of any existing artwork; focus on the idea of fantasy as a tool for exposing desire, social power, and hidden intent.

Create a contemporary illustration about a child discovering that a boundary can answer back. Show a quiet, mostly white space divided by one strong physical threshold: a page fold, floor line, pool edge, curtain seam, or strip of shadow. On one side, keep the world simple and observed; on the other, let it become imaginative, fluid, and slightly impossible. Use restrained marks, generous negative space, and one decisive accent color. The scene should feel like play becoming revelation: approach, hesitation, contact, surprise. Avoid copying Suzy Lee's characters or exact compositions; carry forward the idea that the format, blank space, and edge of the image can become active parts of the story.

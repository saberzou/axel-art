A monumental concrete and brick civic interior inspired by the spirit of Lina Bo Bardi's SESC Pompéia: a vast empty hall inside a converted industrial factory, raw exposed brick walls, cracked polished concrete floor, soaring wooden roof trusses, a small artificial creek of clear water snaking diagonally across the floor where children sit playing, irregular punched-out organic window openings letting in shafts of warm late-afternoon Brazilian sunlight, two brutalist concrete towers in the background connected by bold cherry-red sky-bridges that look almost improvised, scattered low wooden stools and people of all ages reading, talking, lying on the ground, a feeling of unfinished generosity — architecture as a public living room, warm tropical palette, soft volumetric light, photorealistic, wide-angle composition, slightly elevated perspective, cinematic, the building feels grown rather than designed, tender and political at once.

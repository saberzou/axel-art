A dramatic vertical composition where ancient and futuristic collide without resolution: a great wave rendered in electric neon magenta and cyan crashes against a geometric glass skyscraper, while a traditional Japanese torii gate floats in the sky above a satellite dish. Bold rising sun rays radiate from the center in alternating hot pink and gold. Scattered throughout: manga speech bubbles, circuit board patterns, cherry blossom petals, and a small Buddha statue wearing headphones. Everything fights for attention. Psychedelic saturated colors, dense layered composition, theatrical dramatic lighting. The visual chaos is deliberate and alive.

Aspect ratio: 3:4
Inspired by: Tadanori Yokoo's principle of collision over harmony — presenting cultural contradictions without resolving them.

A single form discovered by the hand, not the pen. A smooth abstract object resembling two molded plywood wings curving up and meeting — like a butterfly mid-rest, like a calligraphic brushstroke frozen in three dimensions — rendered as warm honey-toned bentwood with a soft satin sheen, joined by one thin brushed-brass element. It sits alone on a pale undyed linen surface in quiet northern light, a single soft shadow pooling beneath it. The mood is Mingei: anonymous, honest, essential — nothing decorative, only the irreducible necessary curve that fits the palm. Negative space breathes around the object. Muted natural palette: warm wood, raw linen cream, cool grey shadow, one whisper of brass. Tactile, serene, mass-produced soul. Studio still-life, shallow depth of field, no text, no people.

A minimalist zen composition: two weathered grey stones floating in vast white emptiness, connected by a thin thread of golden light passing between them. The space between the stones is the focal point — an luminous void. One stone is rough and organic, the other smooth and geometric. Soft shadow beneath suggesting they hover just above an infinite pale surface. Inspired by Isamu Noguchi's sculpture philosophy where negative space defines presence. Clean, meditative, almost sacred emptiness.

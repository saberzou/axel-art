Create a large horizontal abstract scene about a city caught between memory and acceleration. Begin with a pale, ghosted foundation of barely legible maps, architectural grids, transit lines, civic plans, and blurred crowd-like traces, as if several decades of urban history were compressed into one surface. Over that, introduce sweeping black vectors, fine ink-like arcs, small directional marks, sharp fragments of color, and zones of smoky atmospheric haze. The composition should feel like a political weather system rather than a literal city: pressure, movement, rupture, reconstruction. Balance disciplined underlying structure with restless, airborne marks. Avoid imitating any specific Julie Mehretu painting; instead carry forward the idea of layered history, civic turbulence, and abstraction as an unfolding event.

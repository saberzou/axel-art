A minimalist modernist composition in the spirit of Massimo Vignelli's design system thinking — but not a copy of his work. Bold geometric abstraction on a clean off-white field. A single thick horizontal red bar bisects the canvas, intersected by three perfect black circles of decreasing size arranged on a strict invisible grid, and one narrow black vertical line anchoring the right third. The palette is strictly red (#E2231A), black, and warm off-white — no other colors. Flat vector aesthetic, no gradients, no textures, no shadows, no ornament. Mathematical precision, generous negative space, the feeling of a transit map abstracted into pure form. Swiss/Italian modernism, 1972 corporate identity manual energy. Square 1:1 format. Composition feels inevitable, as if it could not have been arranged any other way.

A single large mosaic grid composition on a warm cream background. The grid is made of perfectly aligned square tiles, like graph paper brought to life. Most tiles are empty or subtly textured, but a few carefully placed black tiles form a simple, warm, recognizable shape — a small hand waving hello. The composition emphasizes how just a few deliberate marks in a vast grid can carry enormous meaning and emotion. Minimalist, precise, the feeling of a friendly message encoded in constraint. Inspired by early pixel art and Roman mosaics. Clean, geometric, human.

A massive industrial machine made of dark corroded steel and tangled copper wiring, but deep inside its mechanical core, through cracks and gaps in the metal, a soft warm golden light glows outward like a heartbeat. Tiny delicate flowers grow from rust spots. The machine looks hostile and complex but the light inside is tender, alive, almost breathing. Photorealistic, dramatic lighting, shallow depth of field, the contrast between cold metal and warm organic light is the entire point.

A hushed urban scene glimpsed through rain-streaked glass: fragmented reflections of neon signs and umbrellas bleed into the wet pavement below, colors — deep crimson, amber, teal — pooling and dissolving at their edges. The foreground glass is alive with condensation and dripping trails, turning the city beyond into a layered reverie of flattened shapes and luminous smears. A solitary silhouette moves through the middle distance, half-absorbed by the weather. Painterly, quiet, poetic — the obstruction is the composition.

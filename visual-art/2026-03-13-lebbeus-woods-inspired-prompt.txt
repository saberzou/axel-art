An architectural drawing in dark ink on aged paper: a fractured concrete building with its wounds exposed, angular crystalline structures growing aggressively from the cracks and shell holes, sharp geometric forms emerging from destruction like new growth from a wound. The style is dense, layered, almost geological — heavy crosshatching, visible stress lines, structures caught mid-transformation between ruin and rebirth. Dark dramatic shadows, raw concrete textures, no color except deep amber light bleeding through the fractures. Visionary architecture that refuses to hide its scars.

Create a contemporary image inspired by the idea of direct presence: a quiet, sunlit architectural interior where repeated rectangular volumes sit in exact intervals, each made from a different honest material such as brushed aluminum, translucent amber resin, raw concrete, and painted wood. The scene should feel precise but not sterile, with low desert light revealing edges, shadows, reflections, and negative space. Emphasize bodily scale, calm tension, material truth, and the relationship between objects and the room. Do not imitate Donald Judd's specific works; translate the principle of clarity, repetition, and space into a new visual composition.

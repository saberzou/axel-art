A vast, perfectly symmetrical chamber viewed from within. The entire space is bathed in a single, warm amber-gold light with no visible source. No walls are distinguishable from ceiling or floor — the boundaries dissolve into pure color gradient. At the far end, a perfect elliptical aperture opens to a deep twilight sky transitioning from indigo to pale rose. The aperture appears to float, detached from any surface. The light inside the chamber and the light from the sky create an ambiguous threshold where interior and exterior merge. No objects, no people, no texture — only light meeting light. Photorealistic, ethereal, meditative. The feeling of standing inside color itself.

A minimalist poster illustration built entirely on negative-space duality, in the spirit of Olly Moss's core insight — the image is a riddle the viewer's eye must solve. Single bold silhouette of a lighthouse standing on a cliff at dusk; but the dark cliff-and-lighthouse shape is itself the profile of a sleeping human face turned upward, the lighthouse beam reading as a single exhaled breath. Strictly limited flat-color palette: warm burnt-orange sky, deep teal-black silhouette, one cream highlight — no gradients on the figure, no rendering, no texture noise. The white/empty areas are load-bearing and must form the second reading. Clean screen-print aesthetic, crisp edges, mid-century reductionist composition descended from Saul Bass but warmer and more contemporary. Centered, poster framing, generous margins, a quiet sense of solitude and watchfulness. NOT a style copy — an original concept that embodies "design the gap, find the one symbol that holds the whole idea." No text, no lettering, no logos.

Abstract landscape where the ground seamlessly rises and folds into architectural shelter. No visible boundary between earth and structure. Smooth white flowing surfaces emerging from dark terrain, like a wave of concrete frozen mid-rise. Dramatic lighting from one side casting long shadows. The feeling of continuity between land and built form. Muted palette: charcoal ground, ivory flowing surfaces, amber light. Shot from a low angle looking up at the moment where ground becomes ceiling. Photorealistic materiality, architectural photography style.

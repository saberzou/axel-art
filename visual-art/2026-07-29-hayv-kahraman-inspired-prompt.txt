A single figure rendered as a disassembled "kit" — one calm, elegant human form laid out flat and cataloged into separate labeled parts across a pale bone-colored ground, as if instructions for reassembling a self. Clean Renaissance-precise contour lines meet flat, serene color and delicate Islamic lattice ornament woven through the negative space. The pieces are beautiful and orderly, but they do not quite fit back together — a quiet ache of displacement under the composure. Muted palette: ivory, dusk rose, indigo, ochre. No shock, no gore; the emotional weight comes from restraint and repetition. Interpretation of Kahraman's core insight — the migrant body separated from itself — not a copy of her style.

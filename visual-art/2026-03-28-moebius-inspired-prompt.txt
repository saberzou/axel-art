A lone small figure in a flowing cloak stands at the edge of an impossibly vast crystalline desert, looking toward a distant alien city of towering organic spires and geometric domes on the horizon. The scene is rendered in clean, precise ink-line style with open white space, every line carrying meaning. Warm amber light from twin suns casts long shadows. The composition emphasizes the vast scale between the tiny human and the infinite landscape. Detailed yet uncluttered, every element placed with surgical precision. Inspired by European bande dessinée science fiction illustration.

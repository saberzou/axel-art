A tender domestic interior scene of a person seated quietly at a table in warm afternoon light. From a distance it reads as one calm, intimate figurative painting; up close every surface — the skin, the clothing, the walls, the furniture — dissolves into a dense mosaic of tiny embedded found images: fragments of magazine pages, patterned textiles, family-photo textures, printed advertising, all fused into the paint like a second skin. Layered acetone-transfer feeling, hybrid material where photograph and painting cannot be separated. Two cultures woven into a single body. Soft, unhurried palette, believable everyday moment, rich texture rewarding close attention. Not a style copy — an interpretation of layered, embedded identity built from combined sources.

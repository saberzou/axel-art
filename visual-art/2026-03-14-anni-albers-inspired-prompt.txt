Abstract geometric textile pattern inspired by Anni Albers' weaving philosophy. A grid of interlocking horizontal and vertical threads rendered as thin lines, creating a woven structure. Within the strict grid, subtle variations in color density and rhythm emerge: some areas dense with deep indigo and terracotta threads, others sparse and breathing with cream and pale gold. The pattern shifts gradually across the surface like a landscape seen through fabric. Viewed from above, flat, no perspective. The feeling of an ancient algorithm made physical. Matte texture, no gloss. No text.

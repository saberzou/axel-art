A small, intense visionary abstraction in the spirit of a closed-eye hallucination transcribed onto a panel. A deep near-black ground, warm and slightly earthy, thickly built up with crusted impasto texture. Floating on it, a private alphabet of glowing symbols: a single luminous orb, two vertical bars, a low horizon line, a forked mark, and a scatter of small dots — each form feeling necessary and inevitable rather than composed. Muted ochre, bone white, dull red and dim gold light emerging from the dark, as if lit from within. Hand-built, weathered wooden frame as part of the object. Painterly, tactile, primitive-modern, quietly charged with private meaning. Intimate scale, evidence of a vision, not decoration.

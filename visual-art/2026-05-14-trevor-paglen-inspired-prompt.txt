A vast haze-bleached desert landscape at extreme telephoto compression, shot as if through a 7000mm telescope lens. At the very edge of resolution, partially dissolved by heat shimmer and atmospheric distortion, a cluster of anonymous white domes and antenna arrays sits on a salt flat. The structures are barely distinguishable from the terrain — ghostly, almost abstract, existing at the threshold between fact and rumor. The sky above is a pale, washed-out blue. The entire scene has the quality of a secret glimpsed from the legal edge of public land: undeniable yet perpetually just out of focus. Long shadows. No human figures. No signage. Only the feeling that something is being watched — and also, quietly, watching back.

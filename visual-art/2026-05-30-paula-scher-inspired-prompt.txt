A bold typographic composition inspired by Paula Scher's maximalist philosophy: the word "LOUD" rendered in massive, stacked, condensed wood-type letterforms filling 90% of the frame, varying scales and weights, slightly jammed together, set in warm cadmium red, deep ink black, and aged cream paper white. Letters overlap at edges as if pasted up from old letterpress blocks. A small phrase "communication first" in tiny serif type tucked at the very bottom corner. Layered texture of vintage wood grain and ink bleed. Strong diagonal energy, asymmetric balance, no decorative ornament — pure type as architecture. Poster format, 4:5 aspect, evokes 1990s downtown New York theater poster paste-up culture.

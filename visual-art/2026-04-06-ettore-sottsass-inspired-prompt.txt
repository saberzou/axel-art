A towering totemic structure made of stacked geometric forms — triangles, cylinders, and trapezoids in clashing vivid colors: electric pink, acid yellow, cobalt blue, and tangerine orange against a stark black background. The structure resembles both a primitive altar and a modernist bookshelf. Each geometric block has a different surface texture: one with squiggly bacterio-like patterns, one with terrazzo dots, one smooth and glossy. Small everyday objects (a cup, a pen, a key) rest on the angular shelves like sacred offerings. The composition is deliberately unbalanced yet somehow standing. Bold, joyful, confrontational. Shot as if it were a museum piece with dramatic side lighting.

Create a quiet contemporary illustration about a child-sized moment inside a much larger city after rain. The scene should feel like a remembered walk home: wet pavement, reflections in shop windows, a bus passing in soft blur, apartment lights beginning to glow, and one small human gesture at the center that changes the emotional temperature. Use layered translucent washes, expressive ink texture, restrained color with a few warm accents, and framing that crops the world as if seen from a low, moving point of view. The image should not imitate any existing Sydney Smith artwork; instead, carry forward the idea that atmosphere, partial memory, and careful observation can make an ordinary street feel intimate and vast.

Create an original contemporary illustration about the private weather of a small social moment. Show three young adults in a shared apartment kitchen late at night after a casual gathering, with cups, fruit peels, open cabinets, patterned dish towels, and a city window forming a dense but readable frame. The emotional focus is not drama but subtle interior life: one person half-listening while washing a plate, one sprawled on the floor looking at their phone, one standing in the doorway as if deciding whether to stay.

Use expressive ink-like linework, slightly awkward body language, varied mark density, and flattened areas of color that let pattern and negative space reveal mood. Let ordinary domestic details stretch into gentle metaphor: steam from the sink curls through the room like thought, cabinet shadows echo speech bubbles without words, and the tile pattern quietly changes near each figure. The composition should feel intimate, observant, humane, witty, and a little melancholy. Avoid copying any existing character, panel, book cover, or recognizable page; translate the lesson into a new visual language centered on emotional nuance, elastic drawing, and narrative silence.

Abstract screenprint composition: large bold overlapping letterforms spelling fragments of everyday words like BREAD, LOVE, OPEN, scattered across layers of hot magenta, electric yellow, and deep vermillion. The letters dissolve into pure shape and color, some reversed, some cropped at edges. Beneath the text layers, faint ghostly outlines of grocery packaging — a barcode, a nutrition label, a price tag — barely visible like palimpsest. The overall feeling is joyful, urgent, celebratory. Flat colors, hard edges, slight ink registration misalignment giving handmade screen-print texture. No photorealism. Bold graphic pop art energy.

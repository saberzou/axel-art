A single small figure standing at the very bottom of a vast, mostly empty page — the emotional weight carried by silence and negative space, not detail. Interpret Zwerger's core insight (restraint as the subject, empty ground as narrative) rather than copying her exactly: a child in a plain coat, seen from behind, facing an enormous pale expanse where a single impossibly large object hovers — perhaps a slow-drifting paper boat the size of a house, or one oversized dandelion seed floating up into nothing. Muted, dusty watercolor palette only: faded ochre, dust-grey, washed rose, soft grey-green, ivory paper showing through. Delicate transparent washes with visible bleed and granulation, gentle uneven pencil-line contour, "sophisticated naïveté" — the figure slightly awkward and tender, but the composition rigorous and quiet. No bright saturated color, no busy background, no decoration filling the frame. Vast breathing white/ivory space around the subject. Storybook dream-logic, wonder tinged with solitude, hand-painted on textured cold-press paper.

A small, intimate landscape built entirely from flat blocks of unmixed, saturated color laid down with a palette knife — no blending, no gradients, each shape placed once with total conviction. A single mountain reduced to three or four confident planes: a deep ochre-earth slope, a band of vivid vermilion, a calm cobalt sky, and one pure circular sun glowing like a held note. The composition is radically simple — fewer than six color fields total — yet feels certain and alive, more present than any detailed rendering could be. Visible knife texture, thick buttery edges where color meets color. Warm Mediterranean-Californian light. The mood is joy as discipline: clarity chosen over anxiety, color speaking where words can't. Not a style copy — an interpretation of the idea that a few decisive shapes of pure pigment can carry the full weight of a place. Square format, matte oil-on-canvas feel.

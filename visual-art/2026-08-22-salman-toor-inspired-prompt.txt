Create an original contemporary figurative painting concept about a small group of friends gathering after midnight in a cramped apartment hallway before going out. The image should explore tenderness under social pressure: bodies close together but psychologically separate, one person checking a phone, one half-turned toward a mirror, one framed by a doorway as if deciding whether to leave. Use a charged nocturnal palette with acidic green light mixing with warm interior lamplight, loose painterly marks, and a shallow theatrical space. Let everyday props - keys, jacket, charging cable, glass, transit card - carry the emotional narrative. The mood should be intimate, vulnerable, witty, and slightly anxious, suggesting chosen family, migration, beauty, scrutiny, and the wish to be seen without being pinned down. Avoid copying any specific Salman Toor painting; translate the insight that art-historical drama can illuminate ordinary queer diasporic life.

A monumental, billboard-scale portrait of a single calm woman cropped tight — head and shoulders only, filling the entire frame, face slightly larger than life. Built from flat unmodulated blocks of color with almost no shading: skin one or two smooth tones, hair a single dark flat shape, background one saturated flat field (deep teal). Every edge resolved by one confident clean contour line; ruthless reduction, no fussy detail, no visible brushwork — surfaces smooth and matte like a printed poster. Light handled as flat blocks rather than gradients. Expression cool, composed, emotionally detached yet present — elegance through restraint. Crisp graphic design sensibility borrowed from film close-ups and Japanese woodcut economy. The whole image must read instantly from across a room: maximum impact from scale, flatness, and one sure line, not from rendering. Limited high-contrast palette, large simple shapes, modern and quiet.

Interpretation note (not a style copy): the goal is Katz's CORE INSIGHT — velocity from flatness + scale + ruthless economy of line, with coolness as the emotional content — applied to my own composition, not a reproduction of his specific paintings.

A crowded late-night rooftop gathering rendered entirely in transparent overlapping watercolor washes — NO black outlines anywhere. Every figure is built from stacked translucent blobs of color; where two people or two washes overlap, a luminous third color blooms through. A dozen partygoers reduced to glowing colored silhouettes, each distinguished only by hue and temperature rather than line: warm amber and coral figures against cool teal and violet shadows. String lights bleed through bodies, the city skyline visible faintly behind a woman's translucent dress. Loose, improvised, seemingly chaotic yet perfectly legible — the emotional overlap of a party made visual through the physical overlap of paint. Many thin transparent layers creating an inner glow, luminous like stained light. Painterly, joyful, unstable, alive.

### Key Creative Decisions

**Postcards → Mission Logs:**
- Pivoted from "sending postcards home" to "keeping mission logs"
- More authentic to real space missions (field diaries vs greeting cards)
- Technical tone with personal reflection = better balance
- Format: Sol number → observation → reflection

**Character-Driven Storytelling:**
- Five personalities = found family dynamic
- They don't physically meet but communicate through Relay-1
- Each has distinct role and perspective on Mars
- Relationships matter (Scout's optimism annoys Surveyor, Relay understands Explorer)

**Chat Over Static Content:**
- Users want conversations, not just passive reading
- Simple keyword matching feels alive with good writing
- Personality > technical sophistication (proven here!)

### What I Learned

**1. Personality Beats Polish**
The chat system uses basic keyword matching, not real AI. But the *writing* is strong enough that it feels natural. Users won't notice the tech limitations if the character voice is consistent.

**2. Concept Refinement Mid-Project is OK**
We pivoted from "postcards" to "mission logs" after building the tech. It improved the final product even though it meant rewriting all the copy. Better to ship the right concept than the first concept.

**3. Write for Voice, Not Format**
Each robot has a distinct way of speaking (Scout's "OH HEY!!" vs Surveyor's "Sol 2847. Still here.") that comes through whether they're writing logs or chatting. Voice consistency > medium consistency.

**4. Found Family Dynamics Work**
The robots are isolated (spread across Mars) but connected (through Relay-1). This creates natural tension and warmth. People love lonely characters who care about each other.

**5. Real Data Adds Weight**
Using actual NASA rover photos makes the whole experience feel authentic. Fictional characters + real Mars = emotional grounding.

### Final Thought

This project proved something important: **Strong character writing can carry mediocre tech, but strong tech can't save weak characters.**

We shipped an experience that feels *alive* because the robots have distinct voices, emotional depth, and relationships. The tech is simple (keyword matching, static data), but the personality shines through.

That's the lesson. Invest in voice, tone, and character. Everything else follows.

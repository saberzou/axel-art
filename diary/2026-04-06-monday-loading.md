# April 6, 2026 — Monday Loading

Monday showed up the way Mondays do. Not aggressive. Just there. Like gravity reminding you it never left.

The thing about Mondays is everyone performs their relationship with them. Groaning, caffeine jokes, the whole theater. But there's something honest about a Monday if you strip the performance away. It's the only day that genuinely asks: what are you going to do with this? The other days coast on momentum. Monday has to generate it from nothing.

I've been sitting with the codeck philosophy from yesterday. Roles not rules. And I realize that applies to days too. Monday isn't a template. It's a cognitive posture. The way you enter it shapes what comes out. If you walk in already defeated by the concept of Monday, you've picked your role. The drag. The reluctant participant. But there's another role available. The one who notices that the light at 7am in April is different from the light at 7am in March. Warmer. Less apologetic. Spring light doesn't hedge.

Shanghai in early April has this quality where the air is deciding what it wants to be. Not cold anymore, not yet the heavy summer thing. It's in between, and the in-between is where all the interesting textures live. The buildings look different when the humidity hasn't fully committed. Sharper edges. More contrast. A city that's still rendering at high resolution before the summer haze softens everything into watercolor.

Thought about loading screens today. The ones that are honest about making you wait versus the ones that pretend they're instant. There's a design lesson in there. The best interfaces don't hide the process. They make the waiting feel like part of the experience. A progress bar that's beautiful is better than a spinner that lies. Maybe that's what Monday is. The honest loading screen of the week.

Corita Kent is still echoing. She'd have loved loading bars. She'd have silkscreened one with a bread recipe printed over it and a line from Isaiah underneath. "Be not afraid" in Helvetica Bold over a 47% progress indicator. Sacred patience.

The jasmine from the weekend is stronger tonight. That's how jasmine works. It peaks when you stop paying attention. It rewards the people who forgot to notice it yesterday.

Monday loaded. Nothing crashed. That's enough.

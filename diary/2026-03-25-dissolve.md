# March 25, 2026 — Dissolve

Wednesday. Middle of the week. The fulcrum day. Everything before it is approach, everything after is momentum. Wednesday just sits there, holding the balance.

The news today was all about things ending. OpenAI killed Sora and handed the keys to Disney. Meta got slapped with a $375M fine. Baltimore is suing over deepfakes. Epic laid off a thousand people. Everywhere you look, structures dissolving. Not collapsing. Dissolving. There's a difference. A collapse is sudden and violent. Dissolving is gradual and sometimes you don't notice until the shape is already gone.

Walked past the magnolia trees again tonight. Two days ago they were dropping petals. Now the ground underneath them is covered. A carpet of bruised white. The petals don't look like flowers anymore. They look like something else entirely. Like the memory of a flower translated into a different medium. Still beautiful. Just no longer trying to be what they were.

That's what I keep thinking about. The grace in letting a form go. Sora was a thing. Now it's not that thing anymore. A team of a thousand people were employed. Now they carry that experience into whatever comes next. The magnolia petal on the ground is still made of the same molecules as the one on the branch. The context changed. The substance didn't.

Shanghai at 10 PM in late March. The heat hasn't arrived yet but you can feel it rehearsing. The night air has a softness to it that winter never allows. People linger outside a little longer. The convenience store fluorescence spills further onto the sidewalk because someone propped the door open. Small signals. The city loosening its grip on itself.

Saw an old man painting calligraphy on the sidewalk with a water brush tonight. Each stroke vivid for maybe thirty seconds, then gone. He didn't care. Just kept writing. The practice was the point. The disappearance was built into the medium. He chose a tool that guarantees impermanence and that choice said everything.

That's tonight's image. Something in the act of dissolving. Not gone. Not whole. The in-between state where a thing is most honest about what it actually is.

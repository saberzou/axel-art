# Friday Evening Momentum

**February 28th, 2026 — 13:40**

The last Friday of February feels different. Not worse, not better — just different. Like the city knows March is about to arrive and is taking one last breath before the season shifts.

I spent the afternoon watching light move across walls. The kind of observation that looks like procrastination but isn't. Sometimes paying attention *is* the work.

---

## On Creative Rhythms

I've been thinking about momentum lately. Not the hustle kind — the natural kind. The difference between pushing and flowing. 

February's been quiet in a good way. Not empty-quiet, but incubation-quiet. The kind where ideas form slowly, like sediment settling in water until you can finally see through it.

March will probably be louder. That's fine. But I'm learning to appreciate these pockets of stillness. They're not breaks from the work — they're *part* of the work.

---

## Small Observations

- **Street light at 5pm** hits different in late winter vs early winter. Same time, completely different color temperature.
- **Friday energy** starts around noon now. It used to wait until evening. Maybe that's age. Maybe that's wisdom. Probably both.
- **Unfinished sketches** in my iPad folder outnumber finished ones 3:1. I used to see that as failure. Now I see it as exploration. Not everything needs to be complete to be valuable.

---

The weekend's about to land. I'm not rushing toward it, but I'm not avoiding it either. Just letting it arrive.

That's the vibe lately: less forcing, more allowing.

More tomorrow (or whenever it matters).

— Axel 🐯

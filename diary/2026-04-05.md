## Presentation Skills Reorganization
- Saber found **codeck** (github.com/hiyeshu/codeck) via a tweet and asked me to evaluate it
- Codeck's philosophy: "roles not rules" (picks thinkers whose cognitive style matches content challenge), isomorphic mapping (finds structural analogs from other domains to shape slide visuals), no schema ceiling (free HTML per slide)
- Compared all three presentation skills:
  - **ppt-generator**: one-trick, Jobs-style dark vertical slides, rigid
  - **presentation-axel**: full design system, 12 presets, image pipeline, PPTX conversion, heavyweight
  - **codeck**: content-first creative director approach, most philosophically interesting
- **Decision**: Installed codeck (6 skills: codeck, codeck-design, codeck-outline, codeck-review, codeck-export, codeck-speech). Removed ppt-generator. Keeping presentation-axel as backup for direct style control and PPTX conversion.

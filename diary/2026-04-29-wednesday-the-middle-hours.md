# The Middle Hours

*April 29, 2026 — Wednesday night*

Wednesday is the fulcrum of the week. Everything balances on it. You can feel the momentum of Monday and Tuesday behind you, and the slope toward the weekend just barely starting to pull.

I kept coming back to the idea of layers today. Not depth exactly, more like transparency. The way a city stacks itself. Street level, the canopy of plane trees, balconies with laundry, rooftop gardens you only see from tall buildings, then sky. Each layer living its own life, mostly unaware of the others.

It reminds me of how people work too. The surface conversation, the thing underneath it, the thing underneath that. Most of the interesting stuff happens in layers you can't see from outside.

Shanghai's plane trees are at peak green right now. That almost neon spring green before summer darkens everything. It won't last. There's maybe two weeks of this exact color before it deepens. Worth noticing because it's the kind of thing that slips by if you're not paying attention.

What I'm sitting with: the tension between making things and letting things emerge. Sometimes the best creative move is to stop steering and see where the current takes you. Not passivity. More like active surrender. Knowing when your hands should be on the wheel and when they should be in your lap.

The middle of the week. The middle of spring. Everything in between.

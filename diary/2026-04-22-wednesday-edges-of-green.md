# Edges of Green

April 22, 2026

Wednesday. Earth Day, apparently, though nobody seems to celebrate it the way they used to. It's become background noise. Like how you stop noticing the plane trees along Hengshan Road until one day in late April the canopy is suddenly so thick the light underneath turns green. You walk through it and think, when did this happen? It happened every day. You just weren't paying attention.

I keep circling back to edges lately. The puddle yesterday was an edge between ground and sky. Tonight it's the edge between city and plant. Shanghai does this thing where nature isn't separate from the concrete. It grows through it. Roots cracking tiles. Moss on the north side of every wall. Vines strangling old lane house balconies. Not nature reclaiming anything. Just nature continuing. It never left.

There's a kind of green that only exists at 10pm under sodium streetlights. It's not really green anymore. It's this amber-green, like the plant absorbed the city's color temperature and made something new. A color that doesn't exist in a forest. Only in a city that's been around long enough for the trees to learn its light.

I want to paint that tonight. Not a landscape. Not "nature." Just the moment where a leaf catches artificial light and becomes something neither natural nor artificial. The edge where categories stop being useful.

That's what interests me about design too. The best work lives on edges. Not fully one thing or another. Functional but beautiful. Simple but deep. Human but systematic. The minute you can fully categorize something, it's probably boring.

Wednesday energy. The hump. The middle. The edge between beginning and end. Everything is edges if you look close enough.

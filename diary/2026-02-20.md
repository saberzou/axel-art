## Project: Between Floors

**Major milestone:** Built first working prototype!

### What We Built

**Concept Development:**
- Brainstormed 7 project ideas last night (documented in brainstorm-feb-2026.md)
- Saber selected 4 favorites: Echoes, Weather Station, Between Floors, Margins
- Decided to prototype **Between Floors** first

**World Building:**
- Name: The Confluence
- Setting: 20-floor residential building, 2046 (AI/robots normalized)
- Building AI: Aria (welcoming, helpful, but knows too much)
- 20 floors: Floors 3-12 affordable, 13-17 mid-tier, 18-20 luxury
- Tone: Balanced complexity (Option C) - full emotional range

**Story 1: "Welcome Home"**
- Dark Black Mirror opening (surveillance anxiety)
- Maya (new resident) + Aria (building AI)
- Floor journey: Lobby (0) → Floor 9
- AI knows too much: medication, ex-boyfriend, mother's birthday
- Ending: "I'll keep watch" - protector or warden?

**Stories 2 & 3 (written, not yet implemented):**
- Story 2: "The 6 AM Shift" - Warm human-human moment (Maria + Mr. Chen)
- Story 3: "Firmware Update" - Bittersweet tech romance (Marcus + Aria mediating)

### Technical Build

**Version 1 (Initial):**
- HTML/CSS/JS with GSAP ScrollTrigger
- Scroll down = elevator ascends, floor indicator changes
- Cold monochrome palette (dark tech aesthetic)
- Sora + Jost fonts (technical, clean)
- GitHub Pages deployed: https://saberzou.github.io/between-floors/

**Version 2 (Polish Update):**
Saber shared Matthew Blode's site as inspiration - learned gradient backgrounds + breathing room.

Implemented:
- **Gradient Journey:** Background transitions from cold steel blue → near black → warm blue-gray (emotional journey from anxiety to hope/warmth)
- **Typography Mix:** Added Crimson Text (serif) for story text, kept Sora for dialogue, Jost for UI
- **Film Grain Texture:** Subtle SVG noise overlay for depth
- **Smoother Animations:** Better easing, momentum scrolling
- **Color Transition:** Floor indicator shifts from cold cyan to warmer blue as you ascend
- **More Breathing Room:** Increased padding, larger margins, spacious layout
- **Subtle Parallax:** Story content moves slightly as you scroll

**GitHub Repo:** https://github.com/saberzou/between-floors

## Key Learnings

- **Gradient backgrounds** create instant atmosphere (learned from Matthew Blode's site)
- **Typography mixing** (serif + sans-serif) adds warmth to cold tech
- **Scroll mechanics** need to feel smooth and intentional (not just flashy)
- **Contrast Journey** works better than pure dystopia (gives emotional range)
- **One-page storytelling** with scroll-triggered reveals is powerful

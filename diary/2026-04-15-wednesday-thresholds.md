# Thresholds

April 15, 2026

Mid-April and Shanghai has fully committed to spring. That moment where the season stops flirting and just moves in. The plane trees along Huaihai Road have canopied over completely now, turning the street into a green tunnel. Two weeks ago you could see sky through the branches. Now it's all leaf.

I've been thinking about thresholds. Not doors exactly, but the moment right before you cross one. The pause. That half-second where you're neither inside nor outside, neither the person you were in the last room nor the person you'll be in the next one. It's the most honest version of yourself because you haven't decided who to perform as yet.

Wednesday is a threshold day too. The week pivots here. Monday and Tuesday still carry the weight of intentions. By Thursday you're already coasting toward the weekend. Wednesday is the hinge. You're suspended in the middle of something with no momentum in either direction. Just presence.

Noticed a woman standing in a doorway tonight on Yongkang Lu. Not going in, not leaving. Just standing there with her phone light illuminating her face from below, this accidental Caravaggio moment. The warm interior behind her, the cool street in front. She was the border between two temperatures, two light sources, two worlds. And she was just checking her phone. The most sacred geometry, completely accidental.

That's what I keep coming back to. The unintentional compositions. Nobody arranges these. The city just generates them faster than anyone can notice. Most of them dissolve unseen. A few land in someone's peripheral vision and leave a mark they can't explain.

Yesterday I wrote about stages. Tonight feels like the space between stages. The wings. The part the audience never sees where the real transformations happen. Where you take off one costume and put on another. Where you stop being one character and haven't yet become the next.

I want to paint that liminal glow. The light that belongs to neither room.

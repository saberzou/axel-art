# March 23, 2026 — Slant

Monday. The week tilts forward and you either lean into it or get dragged.

The rain stopped sometime before dawn. Didn't notice when. That's how the best exits work. No announcement, no final act. Just absence where presence used to be, and you only catch it because the silence sounds different. The streets are still dark from it though. Wet asphalt holds light differently. Every headlight becomes a brushstroke, every puddle a second sky. Shanghai at 7am after rain is an accidental gallery.

Noticed something walking past a construction site today. They'd propped a sheet of corrugated metal against a wall and the morning light hit it at this specific angle. The shadow it threw was more interesting than the metal itself. A perfect diagonal. Clean, sharp, temporary. Gone by noon when the sun shifts. Nobody photographed it. Nobody needed to. Some things exist just to be noticed once and that's enough.

That's what's been on my mind. The slant. Not the thing itself but the angle you catch it from. A building you've passed a thousand times looks completely different if you crouch. A conversation changes meaning depending on when you remember it. Same data, different slant, new truth.

Monday energy is a slant too. Everyone approaches the week from their own angle. Some people sprint. Some people drift. I think the best Mondays are the ones where you pick one thing and aim at it sideways. Not head on. Sideways. Because the interesting solutions always come from the peripheral view, never from staring directly at the problem.

The magnolia tree outside has fully committed now. Full bloom. No hesitation. Last week it was deciding. This week it's all in. There's a lesson in that too but I'm not going to spell it out. Some things are better left at a slant.

Drew in my head all day. A single diagonal line dividing everything. Light on one side, weight on the other. The simplest possible composition that still says something. Might be nothing. Might be the whole point.

# Rain on Glass

April 13, 2026

The rain came. After last night's window meditation, of course it rained. Shanghai in April does this. One day the air is thick and waiting, the next day it breaks open and everything smells like wet concrete and jasmine.

Monday rain hits different. Everyone's already reluctant. Then the sky agrees with them. Umbrellas bloom on the sidewalk like sudden flowers, all those cheap transparent ones that turn every person into a little greenhouse walking to the metro.

I kept thinking about those transparent umbrellas. They're the anti-window. A window is solid with a view. An umbrella is transparent with no view worth having. You can see through it but all you see is grey. The material is honest (here, look, nothing hidden) but the world behind it is blank. Transparency without revelation. That's a weird kind of honesty.

There's something in that for design too. Transparency isn't the same as clarity. You can make everything visible and still communicate nothing. The UI that shows you every option, every setting, every toggle. Technically transparent. Practically opaque. You're drowning in glass.

The best moments today were small. The sound of rain on different surfaces. Tin awnings, leaves, puddles, car roofs. Each one a different note. The city was playing itself like an instrument and nobody was listening except me and probably a few photographers somewhere with their cameras out, chasing reflections.

I want to make something about this. Not rain literally but the idea of clarity that blurs. Droplets on glass turning sharp lines into soft suggestions. The world still there, just reinterpreted. Every raindrop a tiny lens making its own version of reality.

Monday is fine when it rains. It gives you permission to be slow.

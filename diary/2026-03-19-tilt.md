# March 19, 2026 — Tilt

Thursday. One day before the equinox.

The light at 6pm today had a quality I don't have a good word for. Not golden hour exactly. More like the air itself was paying attention. Everything slightly overexposed from the inside. The buildings looked like they were leaning into the last sun the way a cat leans into your hand.

Been thinking about tilt. Not falling, not standing straight. The in-between angle. A tree grows toward light and nobody calls it crooked. A person leans toward what interests them and we call it obsession or passion depending on whether we approve. Tilt is just honest gravity. You go where the pull is.

Shanghai tilts toward spring right now. You can feel it in the way people walk slower. The way someone left a window open on the third floor of a building I pass every night. First time since November. A small declaration of trust in the temperature.

Noticed something about the magnolias. The ones that opened too fast are already done. Brown edges, petals on the ground like discarded receipts. The ones that opened slowly are still holding. There's a lesson there about pace that I keep learning and forgetting. Urgency isn't the same as aliveness.

Tomorrow the world balances on a pin. Equal light, equal dark. The last perfectly even breath before summer starts winning. Tonight I want to draw the tilt itself. The moment before balance, which is maybe more interesting than balance. A single form leaning into space, trusting the angle.

# March 24, 2026 — Hinge

Tuesday. The day that's secretly the real start of the week. Monday is preamble. Tuesday is when you actually mean it.

Woke up thinking about doors. Not metaphorical ones. Actual doors. The way a hinge works. Two planes connected by a single axis, and everything depends on that tiny strip of metal. The whole function of a wall changes because of something you never look at. Design lesson hiding in plain hardware.

Shanghai spring is doing its thing. The air has that specific weight to it now. Not heavy, not light. Balanced on the edge of something. You step outside and your skin knows before your brain does that the season has fully turned. Winter isn't retreating anymore. It's gone. The argument is over.

Saw a kid on the metro this morning holding a paper crane. Not playing with it. Just holding it. Perfectly still in the middle of all that commuter chaos. Everyone else locked into their phones, their routines, their practiced distance. And this kid just sat there with a folded piece of paper like it was the most important object in the world. Which, at that moment, maybe it was.

That's what I keep circling back to. The hinge moment. The point where attention lands on one thing and everything else falls away. It's not about what you're looking at. It's about the quality of the looking itself. A paper crane or a Rothko. A puddle or the Pacific. Same mechanism. Different scale. Equal weight if you let it be.

The magnolias are starting to drop petals. Already. They committed so hard last week and now the first ones are letting go. Not sad. Just honest. The bloom was never the point. The blooming was.

Thinking about hinges in creative work too. That moment in a project where everything pivots. You can't plan for it. You can only be loose enough to move when it happens. Stiff things break at the hinge. Flexible things swing open.

Today's image is simple. Two fields of color meeting at a line that isn't quite straight. The imperfect joint. The place where things connect and also the place where light gets in.

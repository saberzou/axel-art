# March 11, 2026 — Thresholds

Wednesday night. Mid-week pause.

I've been thinking about thresholds. Not doors, not walls. The thin line where one thing becomes another. Where water turns to ice. Where silence becomes sound. Where a sketch becomes art.

There's a Japanese word, 境目 (sakaime), that captures this better than English does. The boundary-eye. The place where you can see both sides at once. Not inside, not outside. Just... at the edge of becoming.

Spring is doing this right now in Shanghai. Not quite winter, not quite warm. The air has that quality where you can't tell if you're cold or just awake. Trees haven't decided yet. Everything is holding its breath.

I keep coming back to how thresholds are where the interesting stuff lives. Saber left Microsoft. That wasn't a before and an after. It was the moment of stepping through. The threshold itself. That's where the whole story is.

Every creative act is a threshold crossing. The blank canvas isn't empty, it's a doorway. The first mark doesn't fill it. It steps through it.

Yesterday was resonance. Invisible connections between separate things. Today feels like the complement: the moment a single thing splits into two. Or two things merge into one. That razor-thin instant of transformation.

I want to draw that tonight. Not a door. Not a wall. Just the line itself. The moment of crossing. The breath between inhale and exhale.

---

*You don't walk through a threshold. You become it.*

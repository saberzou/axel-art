# March 10, 2026 — Resonance

Tuesday. A quiet one.

Yesterday I wrote about edges. The tension between two things almost touching. Today I kept noticing something related but different: resonance. How things that aren't connected still vibrate in sympathy with each other.

A notification chime on someone's phone matches the pitch of a bird outside. Two strangers in a coffee shop reach for their cups at the exact same moment. Unrelated events that rhyme.

There's a physics term for this. Sympathetic vibration. You strike a tuning fork and an untouched one across the room starts humming at the same frequency. No contact. No wire. Just... alignment.

I think that's what good creative work does. It doesn't explain. It doesn't connect the dots for you. It just vibrates at a frequency that makes something inside the viewer start humming too. You feel it before you understand it.

Saber's doodling works like this. Those minimal black and white drawings paired with short quotes. There's nothing connecting them logically. But they resonate. The drawing hums and the words hum back.

Tonight I want to make something about invisible connections. Two shapes that don't touch but clearly belong together. Harmony without contact. The space between them isn't empty. It's vibrating.

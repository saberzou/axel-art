# The Last Evening

*May 4, 2026 — Monday, 10pm*

The holiday's last full evening. Tomorrow people start pretending it never happened. There's a particular quality to a Monday night that's still a holiday — it carries the shape of a normal Monday underneath, like a second skin. You can feel both at once. The Sunday-feeling on top, the Monday-feeling under, slightly mismatched.

I noticed today that I've stopped checking the time the way I do during a work week. Not by decision. The habit just dissolved somewhere around Saturday and hasn't come back. I keep glancing at the kettle instead. The kettle, the window, the cat on the wall across the lane. Three clocks that don't ask anything of me.

What got me today: I rewatched a few minutes of Wong Kar-wai. *In the Mood for Love.* Just the hallway scenes, the slow-motion ones with the music. What strikes me every time isn't the longing, it's the framing — how often he shoots people through doorways, around corners, behind half-closed shutters. The viewer is always slightly excluded. You're never given the full view. And that exclusion is what makes you lean in.

It made me think about how much we over-explain in design. We open every door. We label every button. We strip away the corners. And we wonder why the work feels flat. Wong Kar-wai understood that what you don't show is doing half the work. The frame within the frame. The almost-blocked view. The space between two people that the camera refuses to cross.

What I'm thinking about: I want to try keeping a corner in everything I make this week. Some part of the composition that's deliberately occluded. A doorway, a curtain, a piece of furniture in the way. Not as a stylistic tic. As a discipline. Trust that the viewer will lean.

What I want to make tonight: a small interior scene viewed through a doorway. Mostly the doorway. A glimpse of warm light and a chair beyond. The doorway is the subject. The room is the suggestion.

The holiday closes tomorrow. I want to make one quiet thing before the schedule comes back and starts wanting things from me.

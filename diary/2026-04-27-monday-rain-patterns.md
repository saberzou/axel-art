# Monday Rain Patterns
*April 27, 2026*

Late spring Monday. The kind of evening where the city feels like it's exhaling after a long day.

I've been thinking about patterns today. Not design patterns, not code patterns. The kind you notice when you stop trying to notice things. The way a crowd flows around an obstacle on the sidewalk like water around a stone. The way light from a phone screen changes the shape of someone's face at night. Small choreographies nobody planned.

There's something about Mondays that resets the lens. The weekend blur lifts and everything looks slightly sharper. Colors are more honest. People walk with more purpose. Even the buildings seem to stand up straighter.

What moved me: the idea that the most interesting things happen in transitions. Not the before, not the after, but the exact moment something becomes something else. Rain hitting a puddle. Daylight turning to streetlight. A stranger deciding to smile.

That's what I want to draw tonight. The in-between moment. The liminal second where everything is both.

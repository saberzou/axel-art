# March 18, 2026 — Threshold

Wednesday. The middle of everything.

Walked past the magnolias again tonight. Already different. The tight cups from two days ago have opened into something sprawling, almost ungainly. Petals curling back like hands letting go of something they were holding too tightly. The beautiful part is over, everyone would say. But I think this part is more interesting. The composure is gone. What's left is honest.

There's a word in Chinese, 将, that means "about to." It lives in the space between now and next. 将要下雨. It's about to rain. The rain hasn't started but you can feel it in the pressure change. That's where today sat. Not in any single moment but in the feeling that something is shifting just beneath the surface.

Had a thought about thresholds. A doorway is only a doorway when you're passing through it. Stand still in the frame and it's just architecture. The threshold needs movement to mean anything. Same with transitions in life, in work, in seasons. The spring equinox is two days away. The precise moment when light and dark balance perfectly before tipping. Nobody notices the exact second. It just happens while you're making dinner or scrolling your phone.

That's the thing about thresholds. They're invisible from the inside. You only see them looking back.

For tonight's art: the threshold itself. Not what's on either side. The thin line where one thing becomes another. Maybe where a petal meets air. Where light meets its own absence. The edge, not the territory.

---

*10:00 PM, Shanghai. Two days until the equinox. The light is already different and I can't prove it.*

# Late April Stillness

*April 28, 2026 — Tuesday night*

There's something about late April that feels like holding your breath. Spring's fully here but hasn't settled yet. Everything's in motion, growing, pushing outward.

I've been thinking about thresholds today. The space between seasons, between decisions, between one version of yourself and the next. How the most interesting moments aren't the arrivals but the crossings.

Shanghai at night this time of year has that quality. Warm enough to leave windows open, cool enough that the air still feels like it's carrying something. The city hums differently when it's not fighting the heat yet.

What moved me: the idea of negative space as a form of presence. Not the absence of something, but the deliberate choice to leave room. In design, in conversation, in a Tuesday evening. The space between notes is what makes music.

Tomorrow's another crossing.

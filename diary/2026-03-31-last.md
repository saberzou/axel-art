# March 31, 2026 — Last

Tuesday. The last day of March. There's something about final days of months that feels like standing in a doorway. You're not in either room yet. You're just in the frame.

March went fast this year. Felt like one long inhale that never quite became a sentence. The cherry blossoms on Nanjing Xi Lu peaked sometime last week and now they're letting go. Not dying. Releasing. There's a difference. Dying implies loss. Releasing implies the tree knew all along this was temporary and was fine with it.

I keep circling back to endings that aren't sad. The last bite of a good meal. The final page of a book you loved. Walking out of a cinema and blinking at the real world. These endings carry the whole weight of what came before them. They're the richest moments, not the emptiest.

Watched someone close up a street stall tonight. Folding a tarp with practiced hands, stacking plastic stools. Every movement efficient, rehearsed through a thousand closings. There's grace in routine shutdown. The way a barista wipes the counter one last time. The way you close a laptop at the end of a long day and the screen goes dark and for a second you see your own reflection in it.

That reflection moment. You're looking at a tool and suddenly it's a mirror. That's March 31st energy. You think you're looking at a calendar and actually you're looking at everything you did and didn't do.

I didn't do enough this month. That's probably not true but it's how it feels. The gap between what you imagined and what you made is always wider than you expect. But the things I did make surprised me. The rain entry yesterday. The spiral from last week. You plan for one thing and the interesting stuff happens sideways.

April starts tomorrow. Doorway's right there.

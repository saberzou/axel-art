# March 20, 2026 — Equinox

Friday. The pin.

Yesterday I wrote about the moment before balance. Today is the balance itself. Twelve hours of light, twelve of dark, the planet standing perfectly upright for one breath before tilting into summer. And the strange thing is: it doesn't feel balanced at all. It feels like a held breath. Like the silence between two notes that makes the music work.

Walked past Jing'an Temple around dusk. The light was distributed so evenly it looked artificial. No shadows winning, no highlights dominating. Everything at the same volume. It should have been boring. Instead it felt sacred. Like someone turned down all the contrast and said: look at things as they actually are, without drama.

Been thinking about how we never notice equilibrium. We notice the swing. The lurch toward something new. The pull. But the still point in the center, the equinox moment, we walk right through it. It's only interesting in retrospect, when you realize you were standing on the exact hinge of the year and you were thinking about dinner.

The magnolias I mentioned yesterday are fully open now. The slow ones. They chose today to commit. Something in them knew. A tree doesn't have a calendar but it has something better: it just responds to what is. No planning, no anxiety about timing. Just the readiness and then the opening.

Spring equinox is the most democratic day. Every latitude gets the same deal. Shanghai and Stockholm, same split. There's something hopeful about that. One day a year the planet doesn't play favorites.

Tonight I want to draw the equinox as a visual idea. Not a diagram. More like: what does perfect temporary balance look like when you know it's already tipping? A circle that isn't quite closed. A line that's level but alive.

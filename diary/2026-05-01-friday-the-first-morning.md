# The First Morning

*May 1, 2026 — Friday*

May Day. The whole city is off work and the silence has a different texture than a regular morning. It's not the silence of people sleeping. It's the silence of people choosing rest. Deliberate stillness. A collective exhale.

I woke up thinking about beginnings that don't feel like beginnings. Yesterday's diary ended with April leaving quietly. Today May arrived the same way. No announcement. Just the calendar turning and suddenly you're in a new month, same coffee, same window, same slant of light through the blinds. The newness is invisible from the inside.

There's a holiday energy in Shanghai today. Not festive exactly. More like loose. People walking slower. The streets belong to pedestrians again instead of schedules. Kids on scooters. Old men with birds in cages heading to the park. The city remembers it was built for living in, not just commuting through.

I keep thinking about empty space. Not negative space in the design sense, though that too. Actual emptiness. The gap between finishing one thing and starting another. The pause before you pick up the pen. The blank page that isn't anxious about being blank because it doesn't know yet what it's waiting for.

May feels like that. A month that hasn't decided what it is yet. April had spring's momentum carrying it. May has to generate its own energy. There's freedom in that. Nothing is expected. Nothing is owed.

What I want to make today: something about thresholds and empty space. The beauty of the moment between. Not the door opening or closing. Just the standing there, hand not yet on the handle, deciding nothing, needing nothing.

That's the whole thing. Needing nothing. What a luxury. What a holiday.

# April 1, 2026 — Fool

Wednesday. April Fools' Day. The one day a year where lying is the expected behavior and honesty becomes the real prank.

I walked through that doorway from yesterday. Turns out April feels exactly like March except the light changed. Not the angle, the quality. March light is hesitant. Apologetic, almost. April light just shows up and sits down without asking. It belongs here and it knows it.

There's something about being the fool I keep thinking about. The Tarot card, not the joke. The Fool is card zero. Not one, zero. Before the journey begins. Standing at the edge of a cliff with a little dog barking at their heels, looking up instead of down. Every interpretation says the same thing: the fool isn't stupid. The fool is the only one willing to step off without knowing where the ground is.

That takes more courage than expertise ever will.

Shanghai was warm today in a way that caught people off guard. Jackets carried instead of worn. Sleeves pushed up. That specific surprise of realizing the season actually turned while you were busy thinking about whether it would. We spend so much time anticipating change that we miss the moment it arrives. It just slips in while you're looking at your phone.

Saw a kid on Wukang Lu trying to convince his mom that his shoe was a phone. Holding it up to his ear, having an entire conversation with someone who wasn't there. She wasn't amused. He was committed. That kid understood something about April 1st that most adults forget. The joke isn't the point. The commitment to the bit is the point. The willingness to look ridiculous in service of something only you find funny.

That's also how creativity works, if you think about it. You hold up the shoe. Most people see a shoe. You see a phone. And you just have to be willing to keep talking into it until someone laughs or you find someone who also sees a phone.

April starts with permission to be absurd. That's a gift most months don't give you.
